# HeatGuard-Plus
Solar-assisted wearable heat-stress monitoring system designed to protect delivery riders working in extreme heat.
# 🔥 HeatGuard+

### Smart Heat Protection for the People Who Keep Our Cities Moving.

> **What if your body could tell you when it's time to stop?**

HeatGuard+ is a **solar-assisted wearable heat-stress monitoring system designed for delivery riders working in extreme heat**.

The system combines physiological monitoring, environmental sensing, real-time alerts, a companion mobile application, and behavioural incentives to encourage riders to take timely protective action.

## 🎯 The Problem

Delivery riders spend extended periods outdoors under high-temperature conditions and tight time pressure. Heat exposure can increase the risk of heat-related illness while also affecting operational efficiency.

HeatGuard+ addresses this challenge by helping riders recognise rising heat stress earlier and encouraging actions such as hydration, rest and seeking shade.

## 💡 Our Solution

HeatGuard+ consists of three main components:

### 1. Smart Wearable

A wrist-worn device monitors physiological and environmental conditions, including:

* Heart rate
* SpO₂
* Skin temperature
* Ambient temperature
* Humidity
* UV/environmental conditions
* Movement

### 2. Companion Mobile App

The app connects to the wearable and provides:

* Heat-stress alerts
* Hydration reminders
* Rest recommendations
* Nearby shaded rest-stop guidance
* Health compliance tracking
* Behavioural rewards

### 3. Employer Dashboard

Delivery companies can use aggregated insights to:

* Monitor workforce heat-safety trends
* Identify high-risk periods
* Support worker wellbeing initiatives
* Improve occupational safety strategies
* Track sustainability and ESG impact

## ⚙️ Hardware

The prototype uses:

* ESP32-S3 Mini Development Board
* MAX30102 physiological sensor
* MAX30205 skin-temperature sensor
* BME680 environmental sensor
* LTR390 UV/ambient-light sensor
* MPU6050 motion sensor
* 1–2W flexible monocrystalline solar panel
* TP4056 charging module
* 3.7V 1200mAh LiPo battery
* Coin vibration motor

The ESP32-S3 acts as the main processing unit and communicates with the rider's smartphone using Bluetooth Low Energy.

## ☀️ Solar-Assisted Power

A flexible 1–2W solar panel provides solar-assisted charging, helping extend battery availability during outdoor shifts.

## 🚨 Heat-Safety Alerts

The wearable provides simple, non-distracting feedback to riders through visual/haptic alerts.

The goal is to encourage riders to:

**Hydrate → Rest → Seek Shade → Resume Safely**

## 💰 Business Model

HeatGuard+ follows a **B2B model**, targeting delivery platforms and logistics companies.

**Target pricing:**

* Estimated prototype BOM: **~$60/device**
* Target mass-production cost: **~$40–45/device**
* Target selling price: **$79/device**
* Software & analytics: **$5/rider/month**

Revenue comes from:

1. Hardware sales
2. Monthly software subscriptions
3. Bulk fleet contracts

## ⭐ Why HeatGuard+?

Unlike conventional fitness wearables and traditional workplace safety measures, HeatGuard+ is designed specifically for delivery riders working in hot climates.

It combines:

**Affordability + Solar-Assisted Power + Real-Time Monitoring + Data Analytics + Behavioural Incentives**

into one integrated solution.
